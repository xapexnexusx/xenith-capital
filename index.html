01 BlackGlass · the research system

Not decorated with AI. Built on it.

BlackGlass is the research system Xenith built to pull every position, earnings report and macro signal into one place. AI agents and automated tools work inside it, each with one job.

  1. TracksEarnings dates and consensus for all 50 positions. No estimate moves a decision until the company's own release or filing confirms it.
  2. ArguesEvery material proposal meets the strongest case against it before it can reach a portfolio.
  3. GaugesMarket and macro dials set one firm-wide regime: green, amber or red. A dead or stale feed is flagged, never read as calm.
  4. WatchesEach weekday after the US close, it checks the latest data and reports any change in the regime. It never trades.
  5. DraftsResearch and client letters, drafted by machine, approved and sent by the adviser.

No access to client accounts. The machine cannot trade them or move money; the adviser does, and answers for it.

03 Doctrine

Evidence before capital.

A story is not a reason to own something. The process requires independent evidence to converge, and the strongest case against a material proposal to be answered, before capital moves.

  1. MandateWhat must the capital accomplish? Objective, horizon, liquidity, tax and loss capacity come first.
  2. EvidencePrimary sources, cross-checked. The countercase is tested before a proposal advances.
  3. ConstructionSize, concentration and correlation are weighed for the whole portfolio, not one position at a time.
  4. ReviewEach position is re-examined when the evidence behind it changes.

The perimeter is drawn first. Loss capacity, liquidity and concentration set the limits a position is sized within.
