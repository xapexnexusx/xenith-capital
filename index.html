Our Philosophy
Built on Conviction, Not Convention

We believe wealth management should be as rigorous as the work that built that wealth. Every strategy we design is grounded in deep research, disciplined risk management, and a refusal to follow the crowd.

01

Research-Driven Conviction

Every position is backed by rigorous fundamental analysis. We don't chase trends or follow consensus. We identify asymmetric opportunities where our research gives us an edge, and we size positions accordingly.

02

Disciplined Risk Architecture

Risk isn't something we mitigate after the fact. It's engineered into every decision from the start. Position sizing, correlation analysis, and scenario planning are built into our process, not bolted on.

03

Selective Client Relationships

We deliberately maintain a limited number of client relationships. This isn't exclusivity for its own sake. It's a commitment to giving every portfolio the focused attention it deserves.

04

Radical Transparency

You will always understand what we own, why we own it, and what conditions would change our mind. No black boxes. No jargon-filled quarterly letters designed to obscure. Clarity is a feature, not a courtesy.

Logan Jackson built his analytical foundation in military intelligence, where the cost of a wrong conclusion isn't a bad quarter — it's measured in lives. That training instilled a permanent bias toward evidence over narrative, verification over assumption, and decisive action over comfortable consensus.

From intelligence work, Logan transitioned to law enforcement before entering the financial industry at Wells Fargo, where he managed over a billion dollars in client assets across multiple branch offices with a large team. The experience gave him institutional-grade insight into portfolio construction, risk management, and the behavioral patterns that separate successful investors from reactive ones.

Xenith Capital was founded on a simple observation: most wealth management is built for the advisor, not the client. Logan designed the firm to reverse that equation — limited clients, deep research, custom strategies, and the kind of direct access that larger firms structurally cannot provide.
