05 · Fee-only

Paid by clients. Only by clients.

Xenith’s only compensation is one asset-based advisory fee, set in your agreement and billed quarterly in arrears through your custodian.

  • No commissions. Xenith is not a broker-dealer.
  • No products of our own to sell.
  • No soft-dollar benefits. No paid referrals.
  • No performance fees.

Your assets stay with an independent qualified custodian, Altruist or Interactive Brokers, and your statements come straight from it. The research system has no access to client accounts: it cannot trade them or move money. The adviser does, and answers for it.

Xenith is a fiduciary to every client.

Custodian, trading and fund costs are separate; Form ADV Part 2A, Item 5 sets them out.
