    READOUT // 01

    REGIME: THE POSTURE ENGINE

    Before we choose a single holding, we read the market's posture.

    Growth, inflation, credit, volatility, trend and breadth each feed one dial that runs from risk-off to risk-on. When the dial moves, the portfolios move with it. When it doesn't, we don't trade for the sake of trading.

    • INPUTSMacro data, rates, credit spreads, volatility, price trend, market breadth
    • CADENCERecomputed after every market close
    • OUTPUTOne posture, written down with the evidence behind it

    The dial on this page is an illustrative simulation. It is not a live signal, a forecast or a recommendation.

    READOUT // 02

    TACTICAL ASSET ALLOCATION

    Tactical means we're allowed to move. Disciplined means every move has a reason first.

    Each model portfolio holds a strategic core and tilts across sectors of the stock market (technology, value, industrials, defensives, energy) as the evidence changes, and leans defensive when the posture turns risk-off. We don't hold a static mix and hope; we shift when the regime and the evidence agree, and we document why.

    • COREThe long-term mix your plan is built on
    • TILTMeasured shifts when the posture and the evidence line up
    • TRAILEvery change dated, reasoned and logged

    Weights shown on this page are an illustrative simulation. They are not actual client allocations, model weights or performance.

    READOUT // 04

    MACHINE INTELLIGENCE, HUMAN JUDGMENT

    Machines read everything. A human decides.

    Our proprietary research system, ██████, ingests thousands of data series, filings and price histories. Machine models read all of it, argue with each other and score their own confidence.

    Then a human takes over. Every conclusion is reviewed, anything that doesn't hold up is overridden, and every decision is signed off by one fiduciary. No model places a trade.

    • SPEEDMore breadth than any analyst team could read by hand
    • JUDGMENTEvery call reviewed and owned by a person
    • RECORDEvery decision logged with the evidence behind it
