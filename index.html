// The Industry Reality
What Most Advisors Aren't Telling You

The wealth management industry has undergone a fundamental shift. Most advisors at large firms no longer build portfolios. They select from pre-built ones.

0
Outsourced
Over half of financial planners outsource their investment management entirely to third-party platforms.
0
Model Assets
Hundreds of billions sit in standardized model portfolios — the same allocation shared across thousands of clients.
0
Firm Models
At large firms, nearly half of all portfolio construction relies on in-house corporate models, not advisor judgment.

This isn't a coincidence — it's structural. The majority of advisors enter the industry from business and sales backgrounds. Their training focuses on client acquisition and relationship management, not quantitative analysis or portfolio construction. The model portfolio industry exists because of this skills gap. Pre-built solutions aren't a convenience — they're a necessity when the advisor's expertise lies in gathering assets, not analyzing them.

The result: your "personalized strategy" may be a risk tolerance questionnaire fed into the same model used for thousands of other clients. The advisor is a conduit for corporate products — not the architect of your financial future.
