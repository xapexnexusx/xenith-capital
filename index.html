About the Founder
  • FINRA Series 7, 65, & 66 Licensed
  • Former Military Intelligence Analyst
  • Former Law Enforcement Officer
  • Wells Fargo Advisors — HNW Strategy Lead
  • 5-Branch High-Net-Worth Oversight
  • Proprietary Technology Infrastructure

Logan Jackson built his analytical foundation in military intelligence, where the cost of a wrong conclusion isn't a bad quarter — it's measured in lives. That training instilled a permanent bias toward evidence over narrative, verification over assumption, and decisive action over comfortable consensus.

From intelligence work, Logan transitioned to law enforcement before entering the financial industry at Wells Fargo Advisors, where he led high-net-worth investment strategies across five branch offices. The experience provided institutional-grade insight into portfolio construction, risk management, and the behavioral patterns that separate disciplined investors from reactive ones.

At Xenith Capital, Logan combines this multi-discipline background with a deep focus on technology. The firm's proprietary research and monitoring systems are built in-house, enabling the kind of continuous market analysis and systematic risk oversight that was once exclusive to large institutional desks — now applied with the focus of a boutique firm dedicated to a select number of clients.
