Xenith is fee-only. Its only compensation is an asset-based advisory fee set in each client’s agreement and billed quarterly in arrears through the client’s custodian. Xenith receives no commissions, is not a broker-dealer, sells no products of its own, and charges no performance fees. Xenith is a fiduciary to every client.
